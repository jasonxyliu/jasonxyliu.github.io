Research

I am interested in developing algorithms and deployable systems that enable autonomous robots to reason under uncertainty and solve long-horizon tasks for and with people. My research aims to make robots more accessible to non-expert users through multimodal interaction and natural language dialogue, enabling safe and effective interaction. My PhD work focuses on robotic language grounding using foundation models and its integration with planning, learning, and formal methods.
